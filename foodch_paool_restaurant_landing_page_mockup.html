The Journey of Footsteps

Every Footstep Leads to Authentic Flavor & Family Warmth

Founded in the vibrant lanes of Marol, Andheri East, Foodch Paool (फूडचं पाऊल) was born out of a simple passion: to bring families together over recipes steeped in Maharashtrian heritage and Mughlai culinary excellence.

Our real-world visual identity reflects our roots—warm walnut wood fascia that welcomes you like home, deep teal-green Marathi signage, our signature lavender-purple circular badge, and subtle pink bougainvillea aesthetics that make every dining experience feel like a celebratory feast.
